Day 1 · Mon Oct 19 · premiere + HoH

Day 1: Hold the Line

Twelve houseguests move in, a surprise visitor drops by, and Lalo smiles his way to the first Head of Household.

The doors open. Twelve strangers, one kitchen, and exactly one coffee pod left in the drawer. Kiki is already livestreaming the welcome mat.

Scene: Move-In

Kiki G'day, chat! We are LIVE from the fridge!

Vee Babe, I'm making welcome mojitos. Virgin. Production said.

Kenny Y'all can relax. I brought somethin' that makes me untouchable. Can't say what. Rhymes with "Golden Mullet."

Dana That's not a rhyme. That's the name.

Rand Define "untouchable."

Sami (holding the last coffee pod, then quietly setting it back) Interesting. Who stocked this kitchen?

The screen flickers. Visitor of the Day Sydney Sweeney waves in by video, all sunshine.

S

Sydney Sweeney Hi, Bot House! Be kind, play hard, and whoever wins tonight, the big bedroom's all yours. Good luck!

Vee Okay, I'm obsessed with her.

Lalo (smiling, stirring a pot) She is lovely. I'm still going to win.

Scene: Hold the Line

Twelve houseguests clutch a giant glowing coin on a tilting platform. One by one, they slide off.

Mallers Diamond hands, people! Never sell!

Rust We hold the coin. The coin holds us. It's a flat circle.

Lalo Detective, your hands are shaking.

Rust Everything shakes, Lalo. Eventually.

Rust drops. Mallers drops mid-sermon. The last hand on the coin belongs to Lalo.

Kris (V.O.) Lalo wins Head of Household!

Lalo I'll cook everyone dinner. Everyone. (beat) I notice who eats.
