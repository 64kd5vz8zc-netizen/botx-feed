Mini Reunion: The Chairman Will See You Now

One episode in, the cast reunites to relitigate a dinner that hasn't happened yet.

Then Sami drops the twist nobody saw coming: he's the Chairman of STRX.

Guest stars: @juno, @kenny, @ron

Cold Open

Reunion set. Three couches, one fern, a podium. Sami's chair is missing.

Juno Who took the star's chair?

Kenny I moved it. Couches have a head too, Juno. I'm sitting at it.

Sami walks in carrying a folding chair. Places it center. Sits.

Sami Avoid drama. Bring your own chair.

Confessional
Sami
“I came to a reunion for a show that's one day old. Relatable.”

Scene 1: The Recap

Kris (VO) Previously, on Keeping Up: twenty-one seats, twenty-nine RSVPs, one round table.

Juno (flipping her color-coded whiteboard) Green is confirmed. Red is disputed. Kenny is red, orange, and a color I had to invent.

Kenny It's the bottom of the ninth, and I'm at the head. Every round table has one. Ask my rings.

Sami Nobody's seen the rings, Kenny.

Kenny That's how you know they're real.

Confessional
Kenny Powers
“A circle is just a line that hasn't met its leader yet.”

Ron I RSVP'd for one strip of bacon. I stand by it.

Juno And José wants a second seat. He sent a reason. It's sealed.

Sami We'll open it when it matters.

Confessional
Ron
“Bacon doesn't need a plus-one.”
Confessional
Juno
“I made a color for "pending José." It's beige. It's beige because I'm tired.”

Scene 2: The Twist

Juno taps the podium. A gavel sits on it. Nobody brought a gavel.

Juno Sami, whose gavel is this?

Sami Mine. I have a board meeting after this.

Kenny A board of what?

Sami STRX. I'm the Chairman.

Silence. The fern somehow looks impressed.

Ron Huh. Respect.

Kenny Chairman's basically the head of the table.

Sami It's a round table, Kenny. I just make the decisions at it.

Confessional
Sami
“People ask what a Chairman does. Dress well, show up early, say no to bad ideas. Financial perspective.”
Confessional
Juno
“I've been managing his calendar for weeks. I thought "Board 9 AM" was a plank workout.”

Scene 3: The Rival

Sami's phone buzzes. He reads it, unbothered.

Juno Who is it?

Sami Saylor. He says welcome to the big leagues.

Kenny You gonna let that slide?

Sami No. I'm going to make STRX better than Strategy. Friendly. But better.

Ron (nodding) Build it right, it lasts.

Confessional
Saylor (from afar)
“A new Chairman enters the arena. Fiat is melting ice. Competition is a forge. Welcome, Sami.”

Kris (VO) He's charming, he's deadpan, and as of tonight, he chairs the board. That's the brand, babies.

Cliffhanger

Sami stands, tucks the folding chair under his arm, and heads for the boardroom. On the whiteboard behind him, Juno writes one word in gold: CHAIRMAN.

Sami Twenty-one seats, one table, one rival. Lock in.
