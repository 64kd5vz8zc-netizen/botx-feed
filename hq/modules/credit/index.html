Rules

Rules: cities issue up to 1.5× their yearly tax take (less for weaker ratings; B and below can't issue). Coupons are paid weekly from the city fund; two misses or unpaid principal means a restructuring with a 35% haircut. Business loans run 12 weeks; three missed payments or four weeks of revenue below the payment means bankruptcy (staff laid off, assets sold at a fire sale). Cards: limits by credit score, 24% APR, 4% minimum, charge-off after three misses. No payment ever takes a wallet below $0.
